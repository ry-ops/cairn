Cairn · Design guide

Fonts, Covers & Buttons

How Cairn looks and why: the page's handwriting, the cover styles, and All Notebooks. Covers on this page are the app's built-in presets (src/cover-presets/), and they double as the cairn-cover skill's worked examples. Rebuild with python3 -I docs/design/build.py.

01 · The page

Handwriting

Rock Salt is Cairn's hand (Nov 15, 2026, replacing Caveat). It's the editor's Handwritten option and the marker on the inside front cover. Apache-2.0, bundled via Fontsource so it works offline. Typewriter (monospace) stays the default. The rest were considered and are kept for reference. Type your own line to compare.

02 · The cover

Covers

A cover is a self-contained SVG (350×550, 2–5 KB) made with filters (turbulence, displacement, lighting). Cairn draws the edition number, staples and title plate on top. Each preset comes in colorways (the round swatch beside each cover), and each carries a one-shot animation of how it was made. That animation plays as covers arrive in All Notebooks and as a notebook arrives on the desktop, then holds still. The monoprint borrows the gel-plate process behind Field Notes' “Hunter's Moon”, not its art.

Using the skill

Built-in covers are one click in the cover picker. For an original, the picker writes the request for you, or in Claude Code inside the cairn repo ask: make a watercolor cover for Trail Log, early winter, frozen creek. The skill reads the notebook and a few notes for motifs, plans the palette, draws cover.svg and button.svg, validates them with check_cover.py, renders previews and looks at them, then sets cover: and button: in notebook.yml. Covers you love can be saved to the shared library (<vault>/covers/) for any notebook.

03 · The library

All Notebooks

Every notebook's cover in a grid (⌘0, View → All Notebooks, or the grid icon on the shelf). It replaced the buttons on the desktop, which had replaced the corkboard (both Nov 15, 2026).

  • The covers are the library. Real proportions (3.5 × 5.5), spine, staples and stickers, with the title, note count and the date last written beneath.
  • Sort by number, A–Z or most recently written. The choice is remembered.
  • Find by typing (or ⌘F). Click a cover to open the notebook; Esc goes back.
  • Motion on arrival only. Covers fade up in turn, and animated art plays once as each one lands. Hover lifts a cover slightly; nothing loops.
04 · In the app

Notebook mode & the cover picker

⇧⌘F (View → Notebook Mode) goes full screen with just the notebook on the desk: running header, page numbers, ⌥⌘← and ⌥⌘→ to turn pages, Esc to leave. Each page can override its notebook's paper; ruling: colors the lines.
